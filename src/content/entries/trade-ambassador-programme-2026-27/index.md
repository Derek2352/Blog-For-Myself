## What it was

The **Trade Ambassador Programme** (<span lang="zh-Hant">商貿大使計劃</span>) is the Hong Kong
Trade Development Council's year-long programme for tertiary students, running since 2007 to widen
students' understanding of Hong Kong and global trade. Each cohort brings in members from
universities and colleges across the city, and the 2026/27 year runs from October 2026 to June 2027
in five kinds of activity: an orientation briefing, Meet the Economist, guided tours of trade fairs
and conferences, CEO talks, and company visits.

This term is themed **AI, Innovation and Global Trade**: how AI and emerging technologies change the
way businesses operate and open up new markets. Members earn the programme certificate by attending
three of the scheduled activities.

## What I did

I enrolled in September 2026 and joined the 2026/27 cohort as a Trade Ambassador. The term opened
on **9 October** with the Orientation Briefing & Meet the Economist at the HKTDC SME Centre, in the
Convention and Exhibition Centre in Wan Chai, run by HKTDC's SME Relations, Global Network and
Supply Chain team.

The first part was HKTDC itself, in its 60th-anniversary year. It organises its support for
industry into six sector clusters, from finance and professional services to technology and digital
innovation, and three of its services were walked through in turn: **HKTDC Research** for market
intelligence (business analysis, regulatory alerts, market and industry profiles), **Hong Kong Means
Business** for news on its events and on global trade, and **hktdc.com Sourcing**, an online
marketplace matching more than 2 million registered buyers with over 130,000 suppliers, at more
than 20 million connections a year. Then the programme: the five activities and the year they fill.

After that, HKTDC Research's economists took the floor on the economic and innovation trends
shaping the business outlook, and HKTDC's HR team closed with its Internship Programme and
Executive Trainee Programme.
