## What it was

A selected-participant business-development study tour — run under the theme *"Learning
Requires Perseverance"* by HSUHK's Student Affairs Office — across three cities that each
tell a different chapter of China's economy: **Shanghai** (finance and global business),
**Yiwu** (the world's largest small-commodities trading hub), and **Hangzhou** (home of
Alibaba and China's digital-economy engine).

## What I did

Across the three cities I analysed market trends and looked specifically for **cross-border
collaboration openings in FinTech and the digital economy**, in dialogue with industry
leaders and mainland peers. Seeing Shanghai's finance, Yiwu's physical trade, and Hangzhou's
digital platforms back to back was a live lesson in how those layers stack into one economy —
and where Hong Kong plugs in.

## What I learned

The tour built the foundation I'd draw on all year: a working sense of **international
business practice and cross-cultural communication** that a classroom can't give. Yiwu in
particular reframed "digital economy" for me — behind every platform is still a mountain of
real goods and real traders, and the interesting FinTech questions live at that seam between
the physical and the digital.
