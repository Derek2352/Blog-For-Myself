## What it was

**"From Cherry to Cup – Ai"** is a short AI-generated film that placed **third globally** in
the 2025 AI Video for Business Competition run by Alibaba Cloud. The brief was business
storytelling — turn a brand narrative into something people actually want to watch — and the
piece traces a coffee's journey from cherry to cup as a way into that story.

## What I did

I built the film with Alibaba Cloud's generative stack — **Qwen** for language and **Wan
2.5** for image-to-video synthesis. Wan 2.5 generates synchronised visuals and audio in a
single pass, so the craft moves upstream into the prompt: shot description, pacing, mood,
and continuity all have to be specified precisely enough that separately generated frames
read as one coherent piece. That's the real work — treating **prompt engineering as
direction**, iterating shot by shot until the cuts feel intentional rather than assembled.

The judging weighed production quality, promotional usability, and social-media engagement,
so I designed for a real audience, not just a showreel: a narrative arc, a brand payoff, and
a length that holds attention.

## What I learned

AI doesn't remove the director; it relocates the effort. The models handle rendering, but
taste, structure, and restraint are still entirely human — and a striking single frame means
nothing if the sequence doesn't carry a story. I also learned how quickly these tools move:
building at the frontier of Qwen and Wan meant treating a still-evolving toolchain as a
creative partner and staying comfortable when the ground shifted under me.

There's a through-line to the rest of my work here too: the same instinct that reads a
business narrative for a pitch reads it for a film.
