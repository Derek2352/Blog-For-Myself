## What it was

The **NextGen Video Challenge: Powered by AI**, run by the HSUHK School of Business, asked
students to use generative AI to make video content that works as real digital-media
marketing — not just a tech demo. I won **First Runner-up in the Individual Category**, and
the result came with two things that outlasted the contest: a formal **Recommendation
Letter signed by the Dean of the School of Business**, and a place on a fully-covered study
tour across **Macao, Zhuhai, Guangzhou and Shenzhen**.

## What I did

I produced award-winning video content with generative-AI tools, but the point the judges
rewarded was integration — pairing the AI craft with an actual **digital-media marketing
strategy**, so the piece had a purpose and an audience rather than just a look. This was an
early proof of the creative direction I'd carry into later, bigger AI-film competitions.

## What I learned

Being recognised *on merit* — enough to earn a Dean's letter and a selected place on the
study tour — taught me that consistent, purposeful work compounds: one result opens the next
door. It also confirmed that my edge isn't the tool, it's the judgment around it — knowing
what a piece is *for*.
