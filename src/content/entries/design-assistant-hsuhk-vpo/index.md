## What it was

A part-time role with HSUHK's **Office of the Vice-President (Learning & Student
Experience)** — the university office responsible for the academic and career experience of
students. My brief was to bring AI into the office's design and data work in a way that was
faster *and* accountable.

## What I did

Three threads, each with a measurable point:

- **An AI-integrated design process** to rapidly prototype web visuals for the Academic and
  Career modules — cutting production lead times without cutting quality.
- **Standardised documentation** of the AI prompt logic and design system, so the work hit
  **100% brand alignment and full process traceability** — deliberately mirroring explainable-AI
  practice, where you can always show *why* an output looks the way it does.
- **Digitised 300+ bilingual professional records** into structured, audit-ready datasets,
  keeping confidentiality and data accuracy intact throughout.

## What I learned

The real lesson was that in an institution, **traceability is the feature**. Anyone can
generate a visual with AI; the value I added was making the process auditable and
on-brand every time, so the office could trust it and repeat it. Handling 300+ bilingual
records also drilled in the unglamorous discipline behind good data — accuracy and
confidentiality first — which is exactly the rigour finance work demands.
