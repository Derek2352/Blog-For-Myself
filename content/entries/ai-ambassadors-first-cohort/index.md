## What it was

I was selected for the **first-ever cohort** of the HSUHK School of Business **"#BE
AI-Ready" AI Ambassadors Programme** — a pioneering team asked to cultivate a genuine
*Business + AI* culture across the School, running through an expected May 2027. Being in
the inaugural cohort means helping define what the role even is.

## What I did

The core of the work is **translation**: taking advanced, hands-on expertise — cloud
architecture, generative AI, local/self-hosted LLMs — and turning it into knowledge that's
actually accessible to faculty and peers who don't live in those tools. I work alongside the
School to explore and integrate AI into everyday learning and campus life, treating the
technology as a collaborative partner rather than a gadget to show off.

## What I learned

Advocacy is a different skill from building. It's not enough to *know* how a local LLM works;
the job is to make someone else feel it's within their reach — which forces a much deeper,
clearer understanding than solo work ever does. Being trusted with a first cohort also comes
with the responsibility of setting a tone others will follow.
